Audio Files for Spelling Words
==============================

Place audio files for spelling words in this directory.

Naming Convention:
  {word-lowercase}.mp3

Example:
  If the spelling word is "BEAUTIFUL", save the audio file as:
    beautiful.mp3

The app will try to load /audio/{word-lowercase}.mp3 for each spelling word during the game.
If the audio file is missing or fails to load, the app will fall back to text-to-speech (TTS).

Supported Formats:
  - MP3
  - WAV
  - OGG
  - M4A

Tips:
  - Keep audio files short (1-2 seconds)
  - Speak clearly and at a natural pace
  - Use a microphone to ensure good audio quality
  - Test the audio in the app before using it in a live game
